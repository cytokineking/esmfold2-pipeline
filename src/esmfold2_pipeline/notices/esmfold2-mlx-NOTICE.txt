esmfold2-mlx is an unofficial port of ESMFold2 to Apple silicon. It is not affiliated
with, or endorsed by, Biohub.

Several modules re-implement or patch parts of the ESMFold2 reference implementation
(https://github.com/Biohub/esm): the folding trunk, the diffusion sampler and the ESMC
language-model encoder. That implementation is distributed under the MIT licence below,
which requires this notice to accompany copies and derived works.

Model weights are not distributed here. They are downloaded from Biohub's Hugging Face
repositories on first use, under the terms stated on those model pages. Use of ESMFold2 is
also subject to Biohub's Acceptable Use Policy, referenced on the ESMFold2 model card.
